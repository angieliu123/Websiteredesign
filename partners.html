Integration shape

One call pattern: Select, Arbitrate, Log.

SimCore is an MCP server. Your agent calls it before acting and gets back a governed instruction set and a trace ID. Nothing else about your stack changes.

Schemas, request and response shapes, and a worked example

1. Select

Your agent sends the task and the subject. SimCore returns the few Sims that apply, not the whole profile or policy library.

2. Arbitrate

Conflicts resolve in a fixed order of authority: organizational policy, role norms, then personal preference.

3. Log

The rules considered, the rule that won, and the reason are recorded under a trace ID your reviewers can open later.

Where our layer stops

A narrow layer, on purpose.

A partner should know exactly what they hand to us and what stays theirs.

What SimCore does

  • Stores and versions Sims (policy, role, and preference rules)
  • Selects the relevant Sims for a task
  • Arbitrates conflicts by authority
  • Returns an instruction set and logs a trace

What stays with you

  • The model and the runtime. SimCore does not call your model
  • Tool execution. SimCore returns instructions, it does not act
  • Authoring your policy. Administrators write the rules
  • Identity and access control for your own systems
Neutrality commitment

Your partners stay your partners.

Model-neutral by construction

Sims are plain, versioned data. The layer works in front of any model, so a provider switch does not reopen your review.

Runtime-neutral

Any MCP-compatible agent can call SimCore. The SDK covers agents that are not MCP-native.

Evidence you can rerun

SimBench is public on GitHub and described in our paper. We want partners to test our claims, not take them on trust.

Deploy where your data lives

On-device, in your cloud account, or inside your VPC. See Trust for the three deployment tiers.

Design-partner track

Start with one agent and one workflow.

Entry is design-partner-first. You bring a real workflow, we run the layer on it, and you decide on what you see.

  1. Scope

    Pick one agent and one workflow, and share the policies it must follow.

  2. Run

    We run selection and arbitration on your tasks.

  3. Compare

    See behavior, cost, and audit trail side by side with what you inject today.

  4. Review

    Your reviewers read the traces and decide whether it clears approval.
