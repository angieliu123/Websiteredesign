What we collect, and what we don't.

VersarAI builds the behavior layer for AI agents. Our product is designed so that user data stays on the device or inside our customer's infrastructure. This policy explains the personal information we do handle and the choices you have.

Who we are

VersarAI, Inc. ("VersarAI", "we", "us") is a Delaware corporation. This policy covers versarai.ai, our communications with you, and the VersarAI Sims Engine and related services we provide to business customers.

For information you give us directly, such as an email, VersarAI is the controller. For data our customers process through our product, the customer is the controller and VersarAI acts as their processor. Section 4 explains that relationship.

Visiting our website

Our website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking scripts. We do not build profiles of visitors.

Like any website, our hosting provider records standard server logs when you load a page: your IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time. We use these logs only to keep the site running and secure.

Our Videos page displays thumbnails served by YouTube, and our video links open on YouTube. When your browser loads those images or you follow those links, Google receives your IP address and handles it under its own privacy policy.

Contacting us

When you email us to request a demo, an evaluation, or security documentation, we receive your name, email address, organization, and whatever you include in your message. We use this to reply, to run the evaluation you asked for, and to keep a record of our business relationship. Our legal basis under GDPR is your request and our legitimate interest in responding to it.

We do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask to be added.

Data in our product

Our customers use the Sims Engine to give their AI agents a structured model of their users. Where that data lives depends on the deployment the customer chooses:

  • On-device deployments keep Sims and inference on the user's device. VersarAI does not receive this data.
  • Customer-hosted deployments run inside the customer's own cloud or VPC. VersarAI does not receive this data.
  • Hybrid deployments keep Sims on the device and send the context selected for a task to the model provider the customer configures.

When we do process customer data, for example during a pilot or evaluation, we act only on the customer's documented instructions and under a Data Processing Agreement. We do not use customer data to train models for anyone else, and we do not sell it.

If you are an end user of a product built on VersarAI, the company that provides that product controls your data. Please contact them first. We will help them respond to your request.

Sharing and subprocessors

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose it only to:

  • Service providers that host our website, email, and infrastructure, under contracts that limit their use of the data.
  • Professional advisers, such as lawyers and accountants, where needed.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.
  • A successor organization, if VersarAI is involved in a merger or acquisition. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different policy.

Customers can request our current subprocessor list at info@versarai.ai.

Retention and security

We keep correspondence for as long as the business relationship continues, and then for as long as tax and legal obligations require. Server logs are kept only for operations and security. Customer data processed during a pilot or evaluation is returned or deleted when the engagement ends, as set out in the customer's agreement.

We protect information with encryption in transit, restricted access, and the principle that data we do not hold cannot be exposed. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

International transfers

VersarAI is based in the United States. If you contact us from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, your information is processed in the United States. Where we process personal data on behalf of customers in those regions, we rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses in our Data Processing Agreement.

Your rights under GDPR

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data, and to object to processing based on our legitimate interests. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@versarai.ai. We respond within 30 days.

California residents

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose, to request its deletion or correction, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of. We will not treat you differently for exercising these rights.

In the past 12 months we collected identifiers (name, email address, IP address) and professional information (organization and role) from the sources described above, for the purposes described above. Submit requests to info@versarai.ai. We verify requests by confirming the email address on record. An authorized agent can submit a request on your behalf with your written permission.

Children

Our website and services are for businesses. They are not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes and contact

When we update this policy, we will change the effective date above. If a change is material, we will say so on this page before it takes effect.

Questions about this policy or our practices go to info@versarai.ai. For how we approach security and compliance, see our Trust page.
