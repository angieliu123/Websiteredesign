Your users' data stays where you put it.

Enterprise teams approve agents only when they can see what the agent knows, where that knowledge lives, and why it acted. This page sets out how VersarAI handles each of those, and where we stand on formal compliance.

Deployment

You choose where Sims run.

Tier 1

On-device

Sims and inference run on the user's device. User data does not reach VersarAI servers.

Tier 2

Local Sims, cloud inference

Sims stay on the device. Only the context selected for a task is sent to the model provider you configure.

Tier 3

Your cloud or VPC

The Sims Engine runs inside your infrastructure, under your access controls, logging, and retention policies.

Governance

Every decision can be inspected.

  • Policy sits above preference

    Administrator-authored rules outrank anything learned from users. A learned preference cannot override organizational policy.

  • A trace for every action

    Each resolved behavior records which Sims were selected, which authority won, and the resulting decision. Your reviewers read the trace, not a model's guess about itself.

  • Sims are plain, versioned data

    User profiles are typed records you can read, export, correct, or delete. Nothing about a user is hidden inside model weights.

  • Model-neutral by construction

    Your behavior layer does not move when your model or runtime changes, so a provider switch does not reopen your review.
